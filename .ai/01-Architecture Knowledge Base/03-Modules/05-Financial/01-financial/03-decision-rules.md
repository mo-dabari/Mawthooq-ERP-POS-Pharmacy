# Decision Rules

## Purpose

This document explains the architectural decisions behind the FinancialRecord aggregate.

It focuses on *why* the aggregate is designed this way rather than *how* it is implemented.

---

# Why Is Financial a Separate Module?

Financial recording is a distinct business capability.

Operational modules execute business processes.

The Financial module records the financial consequences of those completed processes.

Separating these responsibilities keeps operational workflows independent from financial reporting.

---

# Why Is FinancialRecord a Separate Aggregate?

A FinancialRecord represents a completed financial fact.

It has its own lifecycle, business identity, and invariants.

Treating it as a dedicated aggregate preserves transactional consistency and historical integrity.

---

# Why Is Financial Driven by Business Events?

Operational modules are the source of business truth.

The Financial module should never decide whether a sale, purchase, or inventory adjustment is valid.

Instead, it reacts to completed business events and records their financial impact.

This creates a clear upstream/downstream relationship.

---

# Why Is FinancialRecord Immutable?

Financial history must remain reliable.

Once a financial record is finalized, modifying it would compromise historical accuracy.

Corrections are represented by creating new FinancialRecords rather than changing existing ones.

---

# Why Does FinancialRecord Contain FinancialRecordLines?

A single business event may produce multiple financial components.

Examples include:

* Sale amount
* Discount
* Tax
* Shipping
* Refund

Representing these components as FinancialRecordLine entities keeps the aggregate extensible without introducing numerous optional fields into the aggregate root.

---

# Why Doesn't Financial Implement Accounting?

The objective of this module is financial recording—not complete accounting.

Capabilities such as:

* General Ledger
* Double-entry accounting
* Journal posting
* Trial balance
* Financial statements

belong to a dedicated accounting system.

This module remains intentionally independent of any accounting implementation.

---

# Why Can Financial Integrate With External Accounting Systems?

FinancialRecord provides a stable representation of financial facts.

Future integrations can translate these records into the format required by external accounting platforms without changing operational modules.

This keeps the ERP independent from accounting vendors and implementations.

---

# Design Summary

The Financial module exists to capture financial facts generated by completed business operations.

It records financial consequences, preserves historical integrity, and provides a stable foundation for reporting and future accounting integration.
