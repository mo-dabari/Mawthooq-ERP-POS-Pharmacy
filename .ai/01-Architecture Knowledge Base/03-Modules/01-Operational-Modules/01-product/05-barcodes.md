# Barcodes

## Purpose

This document defines how Mawthooq identifies selling units using barcodes.

A barcode identifies a specific selling unit within a Packaging Version.

---

# Barcode Philosophy

Mawthooq separates internal business identity from manufacturer-provided identifiers.

The system never depends on manufacturer barcodes as its primary identity.

---

# Barcode Types

## Internal Barcode

The Internal Barcode is generated by Mawthooq.

It uniquely identifies a selling unit within the system.

It remains stable throughout the lifetime of that selling unit.

The Internal Barcode is the primary barcode used for internal business operations.

---

## Company Barcode

A Company Barcode is supplied by the product manufacturer.

It is stored to support barcode scanning during daily pharmacy operations.

A selling unit may have multiple Company Barcodes.

Company Barcodes are not considered the business identity of the product.

---

# Selling Unit Ownership

Barcodes belong to Selling Units.

They are not attached directly to the Product.

This allows different selling units to have different barcode collections.

---

# Business Rules

## Internal Barcode

- Generated by Mawthooq.
- Immutable after creation.
- Unique within the system.

---

## Company Barcode

- May be added.
- May be removed.
- Must remain unique within the system.
- Multiple Company Barcodes may exist for the same selling unit.

---

# Historical Integrity

Historical business documents continue referencing the barcode that identified the selling unit when the business event occurred.

Barcode changes never invalidate historical business records.

---

# Aggregate Ownership

Barcode management belongs entirely to the Product aggregate.

Other modules may read barcode information but never modify it.
