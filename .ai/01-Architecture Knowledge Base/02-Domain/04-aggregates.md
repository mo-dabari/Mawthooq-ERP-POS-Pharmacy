# Aggregates

## Purpose

This document defines the business aggregates of Mawthooq.

Each aggregate represents a business consistency boundary responsible for protecting a specific part of the pharmacy domain.

The aggregate list may evolve as new modules are introduced.

---

# Product

## Responsibility

Represents the business definition of a product.

The Product aggregate is responsible for maintaining every rule that defines what a product is, independent of inventory, purchasing, or sales.

## Owns

- Product identity
- Product information
- Product classification
- Packaging versions
- Selling units
- Unit conversion hierarchy
- Product barcodes
- Product lifecycle

## Does Not Own

- Stock
- Quantities
- Batches
- Prices in inventory
- Purchase history
- Sales history

## Business Boundary

The Product aggregate answers:

> "What is this product?"

It never answers:

> "How many do we have?"

---

# Inventory

## Responsibility

Represents the physical stock of a product inside a branch.

Inventory is the only module allowed to change physical stock availability.

## Owns

- Branch stock
- Batches
- Available quantity
- Reserved quantity
- Selling price
- Stock movements

## Does Not Own

- Product definition
- Product packaging
- Product identity rules
- Purchase documents
- Sales documents

## Business Boundary

The Inventory aggregate answers:

> "What stock exists in this branch?"

---

# Purchase

## Responsibility

Represents the acquisition of products from suppliers.

It records incoming stock and financial obligations created by purchasing.

## Owns

- Purchase documents
- Purchase lines
- Supplier relationship
- Receiving process

## Does Not Own

- Inventory rules
- Product definition
- Sales

---

# Sale

## Responsibility

Represents selling products to customers.

Sales consume inventory and generate financial transactions.

## Owns

- Sales documents
- Sale lines
- Payments
- Discounts
- Returns related to sales

## Does Not Own

- Product definition
- Inventory state
- Purchase documents

---

# Customer

## Responsibility

Represents customers and patients interacting with the pharmacy.

## Owns

- Customer profile
- Contact information
- Customer business data

---

# Supplier

## Responsibility

Represents organizations supplying products to the pharmacy.

## Owns

- Supplier profile
- Contact information
- Supplier business data

---

# Branch

## Responsibility

Represents a physical pharmacy branch.

It defines where business operations occur.

## Owns

- Branch information
- Branch configuration

---

# Financial

## Responsibility

Represents the financial records generated by business operations.

It maintains financial correctness independently from inventory.

---

# Aggregate Communication

Aggregates communicate through application use cases.

No aggregate modifies another aggregate directly.

Business workflows coordinate multiple aggregates through the Application layer.

---

# Consistency Boundary

Every aggregate protects its own business invariants.

Business rules that span multiple aggregates are coordinated by application workflows using transactions when required.

No business rule is enforced by allowing one aggregate to directly modify another.
